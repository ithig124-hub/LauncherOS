One night — it was on the
twentieth of March, 1888 — I was
returning from a journey to a
patient when my way led me
through Baker Street.

As I passed the well-remembered
door, I had a keen desire to see
Holmes again, and to know how he
was employing his extraordinary
powers.

His rooms were brilliantly lit.

I saw his tall, spare figure
pass twice in a dark silhouette.
