I rang the bell and was shown
up to the chamber which had
formerly been in part my own.

His manner was not effusive.

It seldom was.

But he was glad, I think, to see
me.

With hardly a word spoken, but
with a kindly eye, he waved me
to an armchair, threw across his
case of cigars, and indicated a
spirit case and a gasogene in
the corner.
