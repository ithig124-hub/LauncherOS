Inspirited by this wind of
promise, my daydreams become
more fervent and vivid.

I try in vain to be persuaded
that the pole is the seat of
frost and desolation.

It ever presents itself to my
imagination as the region of
beauty and delight.

There, Margaret, the sun is
forever visible.

Its broad disk just skirting the
horizon, and diffusing a
perpetual splendour.
