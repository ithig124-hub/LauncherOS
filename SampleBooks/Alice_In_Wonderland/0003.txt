But when the Rabbit actually
TOOK A WATCH OUT OF ITS
WAISTCOAT-POCKET, and looked at
it, and then hurried on, Alice
started to her feet.

For it flashed across her mind
that she had never before seen
a rabbit with either a
waistcoat-pocket, or a watch to
take out of it.

Burning with curiosity, she ran
across the field after it.
