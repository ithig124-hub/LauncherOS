So she was considering, in her
own mind, whether the pleasure
of making a daisy-chain would be
worth the trouble of getting up
and picking the daisies, when
suddenly a White Rabbit with
pink eyes ran close by her.

There was nothing so VERY
remarkable in that.

Nor did Alice think it so VERY
much out of the way to hear the
Rabbit say to itself,
"Oh dear! Oh dear! I shall be
late!"
